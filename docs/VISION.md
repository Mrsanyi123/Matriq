# Vision

**Matriq makes preparation for the Ethiopian University Entrance Examination
(EUEE, "matric") structured, accessible, engaging and genuinely effective.**

Matriq is not a question bank, a video directory or a chatbot. It is a
connected learning journey that takes a student from *not understanding a
topic* to *independently solving exam questions*:

```
Learn the chapter → check understanding → practise → get help when stuck
        ↑                                                    ↓
   revise weak spots  ←  see progress  ←  build a daily habit
```

## Who it is for

- **Now:** Grade 12 students in Ethiopia preparing for the EUEE, studying on
  phones (mostly Android, often on limited data) and on shared computers.
- **Next:** Grades 9–11, more subjects, more languages, other exam formats.

## Principles

1. **Simple on the surface, powerful underneath.** One clear next step on
   every screen. The dashboard stays calm as features grow.
2. **Learning first, gamification second.** XP, streaks and badges reward
   meaningful learning (correct answers, finished lessons, successful
   revision) — never time-wasting or passive watching. Missing a day is never
   punished; essential content is never locked behind rewards.
3. **Honest content.** Official past-exam questions, original practice, and
   AI-generated material are always distinguishable. Demo content is always
   labelled. Nothing unverified is presented as authoritative.
4. **Help that teaches.** The AI tutor nudges students to reason (hints
   before answers), cites approved sources, and says when it is unsure.
5. **Respect for students.** Many are minors. Collect little data, keep each
   student's data private, no manipulative notifications, no endless feeds —
   finite sessions with a clear stopping point.
6. **Built for real conditions.** Works on low-end Android, survives weak
   connectivity, and speaks the student's language (English, Amharic, Afaan
   Oromoo for the interface; academic content in English as examined).

## What success looks like

- A student can open Matriq, know what to study next within five seconds, and
  finish a meaningful session in ten minutes.
- Students practise consistently across the months before the exam.
- Accuracy on weak topics rises over time.
- Teachers and parents trust the content because its sources are visible.
